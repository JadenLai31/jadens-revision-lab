# Answer guide and marking rubrics

Keep this closed until you have attempted the questions. These are proposed practice rubrics, not official school marking schemes. Accept equivalent scientifically correct wording. Award each criterion once; do not award the same idea twice. Written responses require human/self marking rather than keyword-only automatic grading. For multi-step calculations allow a valid method mark and reasonable follow-through after one arithmetic slip, without awarding the final accuracy mark for a wrong answer. Do not infer exact marks from a language model or a keyword hit.
