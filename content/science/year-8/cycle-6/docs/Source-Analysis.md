## Issues register

1. E Q16: the diagram and initial model line show A = 25 cm and B = 30 cm. The later 50 cm reference is inconsistent; use 30 cm and retain this issue note.
2. E Q31: “kinetic energy of springs” is imprecise for the initial stationary compressed spring. Distinguish stored elastic energy from energy transferred to car motion.
3. C Q17: “push” is an analogy; use energy per unit charge as the precise voltage definition. Current is a rate, not just an unspecified quantity of electrons.
4. S uses linear graphics for series/parallel ideas. They do not themselves represent complete wiring topology. New circuit tasks use explicit connected paths.
5. Rebound-height ratio requires consistent reference heights and no additional input to represent recovered GPE fraction. It does not isolate contact losses automatically.
6. The supplied glossary says it was not verified verbatim against STILE. It is treated as a user-provided explanation, not an official STILE quotation.
7. Final school scope, calculator policy, timing, formula provision and relative weighting remain unconfirmed. No teacher approval is claimed.

## Assessment blueprint

Two 40-mark forms are included: 10 MC marks, 18 short-answer marks and 12 extended-response marks. Suggested 45–55 minutes is a practice estimate. Difficulty targets are 25% Easy, 45% Medium and 30% Hard by marks, with actual distributions recorded in the QA report; coverage takes priority over exact matching. The runtime enforces minimum energy/electricity/investigation coverage, a calculation cap and unique question IDs. Repeated commands and recent question use are soft selection preferences.
